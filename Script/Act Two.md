# I Left the Lights On
## Act Two: The Empty Chairs

*Book, draft 1. The README lists every place I had to guess at what really happened.*

*Running staging rule for the act: each loss leaves a chair empty at the table, and nobody moves the empty chairs. THE ADJUSTER is the fear, not an event. He never actually does anything; he lurks at the edge of any scene where Jennifer does anything at all, and writes it down. Later the CREDITORS join him: three figures in dark coats, hunched like vultures on a wire, played by the actors who played the LAWYERS. They never do anything either. THE LANDLORD is different. He writes things down too, in a small ledger, and he does send the bill.*

---

### Entr'acte

*Instrumental: your theme (the "A Warm Future" stems) flows into Strong as the Tide, Nora's slack-key theme. The hook by the door is empty. The coat is gone. The lightbox from Act One glows faintly upstage.*

---

### Scene 22: Open Source

*House lights at half. JENNIFER, now, at the piano with the laptop open. She waits for the last latecomers to find their seats.*

JENNIFER: *(to the audience)* Everybody back? Good. *(a beat)* Before the break, I told you the parts I'm allowed to tell. *(She glances at the table, at the chairs.)* I've been asked, more than once, to keep the rest in the family.

*(She closes the laptop halfway. Then she opens it again.)*

JENNIFER: Which family?

{{song: 22 - Open Source}}

---

### Scene 23: Silver Tongues

*2018. A headache clinic in Chicago: a narrow bed, an IV pole, the blinds drawn against the light. JENNIFER lies with an ice pack over her eyes. NORA sits beside her with a paperback she isn't reading.*

JENNIFER: *(from under the ice pack)* You've been in that chair all week.

NORA: *(not looking up from the book)* We take turns.

JENNIFER: *(to us, without lifting the ice pack)* In 2018 I had a headache nobody could stop. There's a clinic in Chicago for those, so that's where Nora and I were. *(a beat)* That's the year they took my boys.

*(Across the stage, in a cold pool of light, the kitchen table. The WHITCOMBS and CLAIRE sit down at it. A folder slides across to them: JENNIFER's medical records. DR. WHITCOMB opens it and reads, nodding, as if it says what he already thought.)*

{{song: 23 - Silver Tongues}}

*(The WHITCOMBS and CLAIRE stand, gather their coats, and push their chairs in neatly. They go without looking back. Their chairs stay where they are, empty.)*

JENNIFER: *(to us)* I want to be clear about one thing, because they weren't. When they took my boys, I was sick. I wasn't crazy. After they took them, I was an angry, depressed woman who'd lost her kids. Who wouldn't be? *(a beat)* Post hoc, ergo propter hoc: after this, therefore because of this. I came apart after they took my boys, so they decided that's why they took them.

---

### Scene 24: Birthdays

*Months later. The table is just a table again, with more empty chairs than full ones. NORA sets a small cake in front of JENNIFER, lights the one candle, kisses the top of her head, and leaves her alone with it.*

JENNIFER: *(from the piano, to us)* Every birthday, I make the cake anyway. The first year, Nora asked who it was for. I said, "Whoever comes."

{{song: 24 - Frozen in the Light}}

---

### Scene 25: The Football

*The Iowa kitchen. The phone rings. NORA looks at the screen and hands it to JENNIFER without a word. JENNIFER puts it on speaker.*

JENNIFER: Hi. *(a breath)* Is this about the boys?

{{song: 25 - Good Grief}}

JENNIFER: *(flat on her back, to the ceiling)* Good grief.

NORA: Want a hand up?

JENNIFER: Not yet.

NORA: Okay.

*(She lies down on the floor next to JENNIFER instead. They look up at the ceiling together.)*

JENNIFER: *(to us, still on the floor)* Something you should know about those years. Somewhere in all the headache treatments, they put me on an anti-seizure medicine called Keppra. In some people it causes rage. It did in me, for months. Nobody connected it to the pill, because I'd just lost my kids, so of course I was angry. Everybody thought I was bitter. *(a beat)* I was bitter. But the rage came out of a pill bottle, and it took us forever to figure that out.

---

### Scene 26: The Dream Job

*Seattle. A small apartment, boxes still taped shut. JENNIFER on the phone. NORA watching her from the doorway, not breathing.*

JENNIFER: Yes. Yes, Monday's fine. Thank you. *(She hangs up. To NORA:)* I got it.

NORA: The—

JENNIFER: The one.

*(NORA screams. They jump up and down like idiots in the middle of the boxes. Then JENNIFER stops.)*

JENNIFER: What do I wear?

*(NORA glances at the empty hook by the door. A beat. They both laugh, hard.)*

{{song: 26 - Spy in the Boys Club}}

---

### Scene 27: The Man on the Rock

*Some time later. The work badge sits on the kitchen table. JENNIFER, in a robe, with a heating pad, signs a form. Then another. Then another.*

*(A knock. THE LANDLORD at the door, warm. He says something kind that we don't hear and shakes JENNIFER's hand. She thanks him. He goes, and on the step, out of her sight, he takes a small ledger from his coat and writes a line.)*

JENNIFER: *(to us)* When I got sick, the landlord took eight hundred a month off the rent. We thought it was a kindness.

JENNIFER: *(from the piano, to us)* Long-term disability. You pay into it every month and never think about it, the way you never think about the fire extinguisher. Then one day you pull the pin, and it turns out the fire extinguisher has questions.

*(Upstage, a single fluorescent tube flickers on over a desk. THE ADJUSTER sits down, opens a file, and clicks his pen.)*

JENNIFER: He's never called me. He's never written. He's never flagged a thing. *(a beat)* I'd know him anywhere.

{{song: 27 - Javert in a Cubicle}}

---

### Scene 28: The Old Scans

*A doctor's office. NORA and JENNIFER in two chairs, holding hands across the gap, the way JEN and LEAH sat in Act One. A DOCTOR with a folder.*

DOCTOR: It's thyroid cancer. And it isn't new.

JENNIFER: I was checked. After Leah died, they checked me for everything.

DOCTOR: For the common ones. This kind has its own markers, and nobody ran them. So we went back to your imaging from back then, to compare.

NORA: And?

DOCTOR: Can I show you something?

{{song: 28 - Alive in the Breeze}}

---

### Scene 29: The Crossing

JENNIFER: *(from the piano, to us)* This part is hard to tell, so I'll tell it plainly. For four years after they took my boys, I was angry and sad, and that was all it was. Then, in 2022, the cancer got into my head, and a few of the medicines they tried for it made it worse. I stopped eating. Then I stopped drinking. They put me in the psych unit to keep me safe until the surgery, and all I could think was: if they keep me here too long, I'll miss it, and then I'll die for sure.

*(The psych unit, night. A bare room: a bed, nothing on the walls, a meal tray untouched. JENNIFER alone on the bed, very still. The chair beside it is empty: the same chair JEN sat in beside LEAH.)*

*(A NURSE, played by the actor who played LOUISE, comes down the hall and looks in.)*

THE NURSE: Jennifer? *(She's beside her in a second. Then, toward the hall, louder:)* I need somebody in here!

*(Light floods the room: a code team, fast and calm.)*

{{song: 29 - The Crossing}}

### Scene 31: Fine

JENNIFER: *(from the piano, to us)* Here's what nobody tells you about almost dying. Afterwards, you still have to buy groceries.

{{song: 31 - Fine, How Are You}}

### Scene 32: The Pad Thai

*Night. The couch. JENNIFER finishes the last bite from a takeout container, glances at the lid, and freezes. Written on it in Sharpie: NORA'S. Down the hall, a door closes. Quietly.*

JENNIFER: *(to us)* So I did what any grown woman would do.

{{song: 32 - Lawyer Up, Hit the Gym}}

### Scene 33: We Take Turns

*Friday evening. Their kitchen. On the laptop, the service from their temple streams with the sound turned low. Two mugs. NORA holds a pill bottle at arm's length, then close up, then at arm's length again.*

NORA: They print these smaller every year.

JENNIFER: They don't.

NORA: I know.

{{song: 33 - We Take Turns}}

### Scene 34: Hope

JENNIFER: *(from the piano, to us)* This next one isn't mine to sing.

*(She gets up and leaves the piano bench empty.)*

{{song: 34 - Hope}}

*(A beat. NORA goes to the table and sets a small chair beside her own. JENNIFER comes back to the piano. Neither of them says anything about it.)*

---

### Scene 35: The Letter

*Morning. JENNIFER comes in with the mail and sorts it standing up: bill, bill, catalog. Then an envelope that stops her cold. Upstage, THE ADJUSTER's fluorescent tube flickers on, and the CREDITORS lean in with him. She turns the envelope over and reads the return address. The tube goes dark, and they settle back.*

JENNIFER: *(relieved, almost laughing)* Oh, thank God. It's just work.

*(She sits down to open it.)*

{{song: 35 - Before I Broke}}

---

### Scene 36: Break Me

*Continuous. Under the door slides another envelope. Then another. Then a knock: THE LANDLORD, the same warm smile. He opens his ledger, tears out a page, hands it to JENNIFER, and goes.*

JENNIFER: *(reading it; to us)* He kept score. Thirty thousand, when he called it due. *(a beat)* Sixty, now.

{{song: 36 - Break Me}}

---

### Scene 37: Highly Capable

*A Saturday. JENNIFER in bed with the laptop. Upstage, THE ADJUSTER under his tube, turning pages.*

JENNIFER: *(to us)* Every time I post a song, I write his letter for him in my head. I've written it a hundred times. *(a beat)* You want to hear it?

{{song: 37 - Highly Capable}}

---

### Scene 38: The Notebook, Again

*A closet. NORA drags out a cardboard box that has been in every closet they've ever had and sets it down in front of JENNIFER. On the side, in old marker: SCHOOL.*

NORA: Keep, toss, or pretend we never saw it?

JENNIFER: *(already opening it)* Keep.

*(NORA leaves her to it.)*

{{song: 38 - Unspoken Cost}}

---

### Scene 39: Carry Me

*Night. JENNIFER at the table with the notebook closed in front of her. Her phone buzzes. Then again. Then again.*

JENNIFER: *(reading)* Ruth: "Called and got your voicemail. I'll keep calling." *(Buzz.)* Casey: "Mom says call her. Also, call me." *(Buzz.)* Nora's family, the group chat: "Sunday dinner. Have you eaten? Doesn't matter. Come eat." *(Buzz.)* Hal: a link, and "Don't open this at work." *(a beat)* I don't work.

*(She opens it anyway, and laughs: the same laugh as his.)*

*(She puts the phone face down and looks around at the empty chairs. She doesn't cry. She almost laughs.)*

{{song: 39 - Carry Me}}

---

### Scene 40: The Lights

*Evening. NORA sets the table: two plates for the two of them, and two more at the chairs by the door. She doesn't say anything about it. Far upstage, at the very edge of the light, THE LANDLORD waits with his ledger. Nobody looks at him. JENNIFER goes to the front door, opens it, and props it open.*

NORA: Leave it?

JENNIFER: Leave it.

{{song: 40 - I Left the Lights On (Finale)}}

**END OF PLAY**
