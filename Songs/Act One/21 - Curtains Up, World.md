## Notes for the composer

- Classic glam: anthemic pop-rock, operatic vocals, dramatic piano, around 122 BPM. Act One's biggest sound, so the cut hits as hard as possible.
- The cut lands on the downbeat of "world." Don't sing it, and don't let anything ring over the silence.

## What changed from the record

- The bridge "Is this real life? Or just the stage? / We're writing history page by page! / Raise your glasses, hit the lights! / We own the kingdom, we own the night!" echoed Bohemian Rhapsody too closely for a stage show. It became "Turn the house lights up so I can see / every face that ever looked through me! / Raise your glasses, hold them high! / This one's ours, and so am I!"
- "For the kid out there in the back of the room" now has the actual kids, Young Jen and Teen Jen, at the back of the house.
- The second chorus is cut so the act ends faster.
- New ending: the last "Curtains up, world!" stops before "world," and a line from Unspoken Cost ends the act.
