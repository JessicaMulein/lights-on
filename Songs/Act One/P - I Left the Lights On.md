# I Left the Lights On (Prologue)

**Act One · Prologue** · Sung by JENNIFER  
**Status:** Stage draft 1  
**Source:** "I Left the Lights On" (verses 1–2)

**Plan:** Verses 1–2 only. A music box; Jennifer alone at the piano, talking to two grown sons who aren't there. The first window lights up.

<!-- lyrics -->

*(A music box plays four bars and stops. JENNIFER finds the same notes on the piano, slower.)*

**JENNIFER**
I know you heard a story,
and you were young when it was told,
and you needed something steady
and a story you could hold.
I won't stand up and fight it.
I won't call anybody wrong.
I just wrote down what happened,
and I put it in a song.

I don't know what to tell you.
I've never known the way.
So I wrote down every year of it,
every word I couldn't say.
It's all here when you're ready.
Play them out of order, too.
I'm not asking you to believe me,
just to listen till it's through.

*(She reaches up and switches on a lamp. One window of the house glows.)*

*(spoken)*
Come inside.

<!-- /lyrics -->

## Notes for the composer

- Solo piano. The music-box figure is the show's main motif (the boys, childhood). Here it sounds like a toy; by the finale it should sound like the whole orchestra.
- Plain, speech-like setting, mid-range, no belt. Around 66–70 BPM, rubato.
- Let "every word I couldn't say" lift a little. This is a whisper, not a song yet.

## What changed from the record

- Stops after verse 2. The chorus ("And you won't have to knock...") is saved for the finale, #40.
- "I never really have / every single piece I had" became "I've never known the way / every word I couldn't say," so the verse ends on a true rhyme.
- "It's all there" became "It's all here," because on stage the house is right there.
- Added the spoken "Come inside," borrowed from the chorus, so the Prologue ends on an invitation.
