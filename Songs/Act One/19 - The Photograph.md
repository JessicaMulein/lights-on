# The Photograph

**Act One · #19** · Sung by JENNIFER (NORA and NORA'S MOTHER, spoken)  
**Status:** Stage draft 2  
**Source:** "We Take Turns" (verse 2) and "Carry Me" (verse 2); the wedding line is Song of Songs 6:3

**Plan:** 2017. The reunion and the wedding. A chair already set.

*(JENNIFER alone at the laptop. She opens a message. Types. Deletes. Types again. Sends.)*

**JENNIFER** *(the We Take Turns melody)*
Then a whole life happened in between,
and I never said your name,
till a photograph in twenty-seventeen
lit it back up like a flame.
I went looking, half afraid
you'd forgotten I was there,

*(Her phone lights up. NORA, grown, across the stage, phone to her ear.)*

**NORA** *(spoken, no hesitation at all)*
Hi, Jennifer.

**JENNIFER**
and you answered like the years between
were nothing but the air.

*(A wedding, very small and simple. A few chairs. JENNIFER and NORA face each other. NORA'S MOTHER is marrying them. OWEN and THEO are not there.)*

*(The music falls almost silent. NORA'S MOTHER nods to them.)*

**NORA** *(spoken, simply)*
I am my beloved's, and my beloved is mine.

**JENNIFER** *(spoken)*
I am my beloved's, and my beloved is mine.

*(NORA's family's kitchen. A big table, loud, too many people. One empty chair, already pulled out.)*

**JENNIFER**
Then I married into kindness I had never known:
a table with a chair already set.
No one asked me where I came from,
just how I'd been, and had I eaten yet.

**NORA'S MOTHER** *(spoken, pointing at the chair)*
Sit. Have you eaten?

*(JENNIFER sits. NORA sits beside her and looks at the photograph on JENNIFER's phone. She holds the phone up beside her own face. JENNIFER nods. NORA studies it, and nods too.)*

## Notes for the composer

- Two melodies with a quiet wedding between them: We Take Turns (verse 2), then Carry Me (verse 2) over the noise of the family kitchen.
- The wedding is very small and simple, and nearly silent. There's no number, just one line each. If the pit plays anything under it, a few soft notes of We Take Turns, their song.
- Nora's first line is spoken, quick and easy. She's the first person in the show who says the name without a pause.

## What changed from the record

- We Take Turns, verse 2: "and I never said your name, / till a photograph in twenty-seventeen / brought it back to me again" became "lit it back up like a flame," a true rhyme with "name."
- Hal's emailed photo sends you to Facebook to find Nora, as it happened. The woman in it looks like Nora, and Nora sees it too, which is true: your wife agreed.
- Nora's spoken "Hi, Jennifer" is new, and comes in the middle of the verse.
- The wedding is as you described it: very small and simple, Nora's mother officiating, and the two of you saying "I am my beloved's, and my beloved is mine" (Song of Songs 6:3). It replaces Ruth's verse on 1 Corinthians 13, so Ruth's Blessing no longer comes back here.
- The woman who marries you is the same one who points you to the chair in the kitchen.
- "Owen and Theo are not there" is my guess. Were the boys at the wedding? Change it if they were.
- Carry Me, verse 2, is unchanged; Nora's mother's kitchen line is new.
