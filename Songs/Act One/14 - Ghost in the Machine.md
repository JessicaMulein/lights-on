# Ghost in the Machine

**Act One · #14** · Sung by JEN, COMPANY (LEAH, spoken)  
**Status:** Stage draft 1  
**Source:** "Ghost in the Machine"

**Plan:** That winter, 2016 into 2017. Leah bedbound; Jen starts hormones in secret. Move the dinner table to the bedside. Leah never finds out.

## Notes for the composer

- The record already has Broadway bones: tick-tock patter verses, a big belted chorus, and a heartbreaking piano bridge. Keep all of that.
- This draft ends on the brink rather than in triumph. The record's big final chorus ("you burn down the line... I am writing the lines!") is saved; #15 is where she steps out.
- The comic patter has to be fast and clean enough to get laughs, because the laughs make the bridge land.

## What changed from the record

- The dinner table becomes the bedside. "Pass the potatoes, dear, watch out for the grease" became "Here's your water, dear. Here's the pill for the pain."
- Verse 1 is rebuilt around the secret: "a little blue pill that I'm not quite allowed," and "my whole chemistry changes in a coffee cup." Swap in the real details (pill, patch, shot) if they matter to you.
- "Dressed up as a banker on Wall Street elite" became "dressed up as a husband from collar to feet."
- "just smile and nod as the moments outclass" became "and smile and nod till the moment has passed."
- "I'm drafting a spec for a decentralized hive / while entirely forgetting how to feel" became "while I fold the clean laundry and keep her alive."
- The bridge stays word for word. "...And then you step out of the door" and the triumphant final chorus are held, and the song ends with her going back in to hold Leah's hand.
- The outro's "Error 404: The old man not found" moves to the end of #15.
