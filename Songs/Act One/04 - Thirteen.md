# Thirteen

**Act One · #4** · Sung by TEEN JEN, JENNIFER  
**Status:** Stage draft 1  
**Source:** "We Take Turns" (verse 1)

**Plan:** Danny's party. An 8-line fragment of the We Take Turns melody.

<!-- lyrics -->

**TEEN JEN** *(small, to herself, while YOUNG NORA leans on the wall beside her)*
We were thirteen at Danny's party,
and I stood against the wall.
You were the first thing I was sure of,
maybe the first thing at all.

**JENNIFER** *(from the piano)*
We had a little while, maybe,
before I let you go.
I was too scared of who I was
to let somebody know.

<!-- /lyrics -->

## Notes for the composer

- This is the opening phrase of the We Take Turns chorus melody in miniature: half-time, piano only. It comes back at #19 (The Photograph) and in full at #33.
- Teen Jen's half is in the moment; Jennifer's half is hindsight.

## What changed from the record

- "and I couldn't find the words / in a world I hadn't learned" became "and I stood against the wall / maybe the first thing at all." That gives a true rhyme and the staging.
- The second half is now sung by Jennifer, looking back.
