**Status:** Stage draft 1

## Notes for the composer

- Outlaw country on a train beat: Telecaster, pedal steel, fiddle, upright bass, honky-tonk piano. This is the one big "fight" sound the show uses before Act Two, so it should feel like a preview.
- Jennifer's verses are talk-sung with a grin; the Company's "Slow down!" is a gang shout.
- The bridge is the only sincere moment in the number. Strip to voice and steel, then slam back in.

## What changed from the record

- "sure, I looked just fine" became "It must've slipped their mind," a true rhyme with "grind."
- "the adjuster's on my tail" became "a man up on the Rock is watching me." In the show he's the fear, not an event.
- The pre-chorus "Every time the sky falls in, / my numbers climb again" became "Every time I hit the ground, / the numbers come around" (true rhyme).
- "I'm doing promo / it's a concept album, son" became "Honey, I'm all right / it opens here tonight," which rhymes and tells the audience they're inside the show.
- The bridge is rewritten for a theater: the 2 a.m. listener becomes someone in the dark of the house, and "I saved a seat" became "I saved a chair," which plants the empty chairs.
- Final chorus: "you bought a ticket to see" and "I've made a show of me."
- Outro: "Next stop: wherever" became "Next stop: the beginning," to hand off to the story.
