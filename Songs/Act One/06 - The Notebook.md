**Act One · #6** · Sung by TEEN JEN, JENNIFER  
**Status:** Stage draft 1  
**Source:** "Where Words Stop," "The Approach to Infinity"

**Plan:** Senior year, at the private school she was sent to. The "I want" song, stitched from two school assignments she wrote there.

## Notes for the composer

- The "I want" song. It starts as Where Words Stop (piano and cello, small) and turns into The Approach to Infinity. On the record that's synth-pop; here it's piano with a pulsing left hand that grows into the strings.
- "to fly up and up into the azure sky" is the wings motif. It comes back in Unspoken Cost ("You got your wings for five minutes").
- Jennifer's outro sits over Teen Jen's last line; the two voices should overlap for a bar or two.

## What changed from the record

- The two school pieces are stitched into one number.
- "not because of permission / but because words can't describe" became "not because they won't allow it, / but the words stay in my head," to rhyme with "said."
- "I'm unique and no one knows / all that floats inside my quiet head" became four new lines: "I'm the only one I know of / who is built the way I'm built, / and I don't know what to call it, / so I carry it like guilt." These are my words, not yours. Replace them if they don't sound like seventeen-year-old you.
- Chorus: "I'm torn apart, I'm losing my heart / I wish I could just explain" became "I'm torn in two, and I don't know who / would listen anyway."
- "What I'd give just to fade away... to leave this world, to fly away!" became "What I'd give to slip away... to leave this ground and fly!" On stage, coming from a teenager, "leave this world" reads as wanting to die rather than wanting to be free. Restore it if you want the darker reading.
- The bridge is condensed to four lines.
- The outro goes to Jennifer, now, so "even if it was only in my mind" lands as hindsight; I added "for years and years."
- It's set in senior year, alone at a desk at the private school, which is where you actually wrote these. The earlier draft put it at Ruth's kitchen table with spoken lines for Ruth and Casey; those are gone.
