# Road Less Traveled

**Act One · #7** · Sung by TEEN JEN  
**Status:** Stage draft 1  
**Source:** "Road Less Traveled"

**Plan:** Senior year, three years after the accident. She reads her English assignment aloud to nobody. Keep it raw and seventeen; fix "in tact." Act Two answers it.

<!-- lyrics -->

*(TEEN JEN reads from the notebook. The first verse is spoken over a few piano chords; the music gathers under her until she's singing.)*

**TEEN JEN** *(reading)*
Here I stand, on the precipice of eternity,
gazing out across the vast expanse that is me,
having to decide in one night, forever, who I will be,
looking into the future to decide what to do with my past.

*(sung)*
Behind me lies the life of a man,
morals intact, skills in demand.
Ahead is a fork in the road,
and I will take the road less traveled.

Few mortals dare cross the threshold,
dare approach the limits of tolerance,
dare cross the line into the unknown,
and this is where I must boldly go.

The answer lies in plain view,
but the question begs to be forgotten.
To one side lies happiness at unspoken cost.
On the other, innocence lost.

Behind me lies the life of a man,
morals intact, skills in demand.
Ahead is a fork in the road,
and I will take the road less traveled.

*(The music swells: strings, a low drum.)*

Wingèd time with its outstretched talons
pulls me ever onward, affording me no rest.
Oh, sweet night, hide me in your cloaked arms
and deliver me into the heart of darkness.

*(The music falls away. Spoken, quietly.)*

Time takes its horrid toll, each chime of its bell,
turning and turning in the widening gyre.
'Twas nobler to bear the slings and arrows
than to hide sorrow under happiness for eternity.

*(She closes the notebook.)*

## Notes for the composer

- This one breaks the rhyme rule on purpose: it's a seventeen-year-old's poem, read aloud, and it should sound like one. Set the verses close to speech, and give only the chorus a real tune.
- Strings and a low drum under the bridge; then everything drops out for the spoken ending.
- Unspoken Cost (#38) quotes this poem back to her, so keep these phrases exactly as they are: "behind me lies the life of a man," "morals intact," "happiness at unspoken cost," "innocence lost," "hide me," and "nobler to bear the slings and arrows."

## What changed from the record

- "in tact" became "intact."
- Capital letters and punctuation are added so an actor can read it. The words are otherwise yours, as written.
- The staging is new: Teen Jen reads it aloud to herself, alone, starting in speech and moving into song. She turns it in, and nobody asks which road.
