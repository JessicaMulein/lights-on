# Another Night

**Act One · #12** · Sung by JENNIFER, JEN, LOUISE  
**Status:** Stage draft 1  
**Source:** the same earlier song as #3 (verse 5, final verse, and outro)

**Plan:** February 2016. Louise's last phone call. Jen never told her who she was, and she didn't feel she needed to. Louise's death is also what frees her to begin. Louise's chair is the first to go empty.

<!-- lyrics -->

*(Across the stage, in a small room in a care home, LOUISE sits in her cardigan, the phone cord stretched as far as it goes.)*

**JENNIFER** *(from the piano, to the tune of Louise's chorus, slow)*
She wanted to die at home, where she'd be near him.
She said she wanted to go where she knew she'd find him.
They'd moved her somewhere she could be cared for.
She hated every minute, and she never made a fuss.
Then one night she called me, and her voice sounded tired.

**LOUISE** *(on the phone)*
I don't think I can make it another night.

**JEN**
It's okay.
I love you.

*(A pause. JEN opens her mouth to tell her something else, and doesn't.)*

**LOUISE** *(barely)*
Walt's waiting.

**JEN**
I know.

**JENNIFER**
She didn't make it another night.

*(LOUISE sets the phone gently in its cradle. At the kitchen table, her chair is the first to go empty.)*

There once was a woman who lived her whole life
with her chin held high and her heart open wide,
believing the people she loved never really die.
And somewhere beyond what I can see tonight,
a woman named Louise walked into the light,
and if Walt was waiting, like he promised he'd be,
I hope he held her close
the way she always believed.

*(The music thins to the piano. JEN alone at the table.)*

**JENNIFER**
I never told her, and I didn't need to.
She loved me the way that she knew how.
And when she was gone, the house got quiet,
and the quiet said, *It's now.*
One less heart I was scared of breaking,
one less face I'd have to brave.
I'm ashamed of how much lighter it got,
and I'm grateful for the room she gave.

*(JEN looks at the coat hook by the door, still empty. The music box plays two notes and stops.)*

## Notes for the composer

- A reprise of Louise (#3): the same chorus melody, slowed to a hymn, piano alone. Your piano version of the original is the model.
- Louise's line on the phone is spoken, not sung.
- The last eight lines are the turn of the act. Keep them plain and unhurried, with no swell. "It's now" leads straight into #14.

## What changed from the record

- The narration keeps your verse 5 and final-verse lines almost word for word, because they were already right. Louise is now the one who says "I don't think I can make it another night," on the phone.
- "Walt's waiting" / "I know" is new.
- The outro is yours, with the names changed (with the stage names Louise and Walt), and "She fought for the broken" is trimmed for length.
- The last eight lines are mine, built from what you told me: you never told her, you didn't feel you needed to, and her passing freed you. Check that they say it the way you'd say it.
