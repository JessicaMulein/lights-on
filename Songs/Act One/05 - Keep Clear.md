## Notes for the composer

- Keep the record's world: felt piano, solo cello, chamber strings, D minor, around 68 BPM. Build slowly to the final chorus, then collapse to near silence.
- The bridge doesn't rhyme. On stage that works because it's speech; set it almost spoken.
- The impact shouldn't be a sound effect over the music. Let the music stop.

## What changed from the record

- The lyric is almost untouched; it was already a scene.
- The voices are split: Teen Jen lives it, and Jennifer remembers it. The bridge is Jennifer singing to her younger self.
- "Look what you did" is now spoken by the EMT, and she isn't kind about it.
- The last two lines are split: Teen Jen says "Keep clear," and Jennifer answers "It was."
