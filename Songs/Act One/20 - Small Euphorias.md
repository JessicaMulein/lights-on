# Small Euphorias

**Act One · #20** · Sung by JENNIFER, FRIENDS (a NURSE, spoken)  
**Status:** Stage draft 1  
**Source:** "Small Euphorias"

**Plan:** Little lights. A waiting room calls "Jennifer." Could merge into #21.

<!-- lyrics -->

*(A montage. A coffee counter. A hardware store. A dressing room. A doctor's waiting room. JENNIFER drops a coin into a jar at the end of each one.)*

**JENNIFER**
Seven-thirty, coffee line,
she wrote my name and spelled it right,
didn't blink and didn't stumble,
and I carried that cup like a light.
Then the guy at the hardware counter
called me "ma'am" like it was plain,
and I sat out in the parking lot
just to feel it once again.

Nobody threw a parade.
Nobody rang a bell.
The barista just got it right today,
and I'm doing pretty well.
Small euphorias,
little lights you carry home.
Nobody else would notice.
That's how you know they're your own.

*(Her FRIENDS crowd in: a trans guy with his phone held out, and a friend in a new dress.)*

My buddy sent a photo
of three hairs on his chin,
like a prospector with nuggets:
"Look what's coming in!"
And my friend found a dress that fit her,
first try, right off the rack.
She spun around the dressing room
and sent me twelve pictures back.

**JENNIFER and FRIENDS**
Nobody threw a parade.
Nobody rang a bell.
He just texted me his chin hairs,
and we're all doing well.
Small euphorias,
little lights you carry home.
Nobody else would notice.
That's how you know they're your own.

*(A waiting room. A NURSE with a clipboard.)*

**NURSE** *(spoken)*
Jennifer?

**JENNIFER**
First time in a waiting room
they called the name I chose,
I stood up like a queen,
and I didn't care who knows.
And some Tuesday, out of nowhere,
the mirror didn't fight,
and I stood there with my toothbrush,
grinning half the night.

*(softer, piano and voice)*
It isn't always like this.
Some days are long and gray.
So I'm saving every one of these
like coins in a jar,
and on the days you can't find any,
you can borrow from mine.

**JENNIFER and FRIENDS** *(full, joyful)*
Nobody threw a parade.
Nobody rang a bell.
But we're keeping every little thing
we earned so well.
Small euphorias,
little lights you carry home.
On the bad days, borrow mine.
You don't have to shine alone.

## Notes for the composer

- Warm and luminous: felt piano, string quartet, a gentle pulse, around 92 BPM. Each chorus opens a little brighter.
- The jar of coins is a real prop. It comes back in Act Two, when she needs to borrow from it.

## What changed from the record

- Almost nothing; it was already a scene.
- The waiting-room nurse now says "Jennifer?" out loud, the payoff of the name that started at the mirror in #2.
- The handclap fade-out ("Spelled it right. Called my name...") is cut, so the song can run straight into #21.
- The record's "home / own" rhyme stays. It's a slant rhyme, but it sings.
