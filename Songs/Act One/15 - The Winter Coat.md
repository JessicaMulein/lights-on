# The Winter Coat

**Act One · #15** · Sung by JENNIFER, DR. and MRS. WHITCOMB, RUTH  
**Status:** Stage draft 1  
**Source:** "The Winter Coat," and "There You Are" (the bridge, verse 3, and final chorus)

**Plan:** That same winter, while Leah is dying. She comes out again ("Again?"). Ruth takes her hand: "There you are." The coat comes off.

<!-- lyrics -->

*(Upstage, in her own house, LEAH sleeps in the hospital bed.)*

**JENNIFER**
I had a perfect picture. I had a sturdy frame.
But every time I smiled, I felt a quiet shame,
like walking in a pair of shoes a couple sizes small
while everyone is clapping 'cause you haven't taken a fall.
You can't explain the feeling. You can't explain the ache.
You just know you're bending, and you're finally gonna break.

So what do you do when the mirror is a lie?
Do you keep the peace for them, or do you fight to stay alive?

Imagine wearing a winter coat in the blazing summer sun,
and everybody tells you that you're the lucky one.
Imagine holding your breath for half of your life
just to play the part, just to avoid the knife.
I didn't change who I was to put on a show.
I changed everything because I just... know.
You take off the coat, or you suffocate in the heat.
I just wanted to stand on my own two feet.

**MRS. WHITCOMB**
Again?

**DR. WHITCOMB**
Are you sure?

**MRS. WHITCOMB**
Is it real?

**JENNIFER**
Like I need a committee to tell me how I feel.
But you don't ask the drowning if they really need the air.
You don't ask the freezing if they have a spark to spare.

*(She looks upstage, toward LEAH asleep.)*

The hardest part was knowing I might break somebody's heart,
but the only way to save myself was tearing it apart.

*(DR. WHITCOMB stands, and walks out of the room without a word. MRS. WHITCOMB looks at her hands.)*

*(RUTH's kitchen. The cow cookie jar. Late. RUTH, older now, in her bathrobe. JENNIFER in the doorway, still in the coat.)*

**JENNIFER** *(now, from the piano)*
Then one night she said it out loud,
just her name and nothing more,

**JENNIFER** *(to RUTH, spoken)*
Jennifer.

**JENNIFER** *(now)*
and the whole house held its breath,
like the wind had found the door.
Some folks pulled the curtains closed
and never came back around,
but someone took her by the hand

**RUTH** *(taking her hand)*
There you are.

*(JENNIFER unbuttons the coat, all the way down, for the first time since she put it on. It falls open. She doesn't take it off.)*

**JENNIFER**
It wasn't some big firework. It wasn't a parade.
It was just the quiet peace of the choice that I made.
It was waking up normal.
It was letting out the breath.
It was choosing to live,
and stepping back from the edge.

Now there's a girl in all the photographs
with that same old crooked grin,
standing easy in the flash of light
like she's always been.

**JENNIFER and RUTH**
There once was a girl, she was always a girl,
and look at her now, watch those wings unfurl,
'cause you can't cage a gender
any more than you can cage a ghost,
but she's not haunting anymore.
She's here. She's real. She's home.

*(The music settles. JENNIFER looks down at the coat, hanging open.)*

**JENNIFER** *(spoken, to us, deadpan)*
Error 404: the old man not found.

## Notes for the composer

- Two songs joined. The Winter Coat (acoustic, driving, building to a full-band chorus) carries the scene with her parents. Then the music drops out, and There You Are's 6/8 lullaby world comes back for Ruth's kitchen, with the music-box figure.
- The final chorus finishes the There You Are chorus that broke off in #2.
- Only one chorus of The Winter Coat; its second chorus is cut, so the scene moves.

## What changed from the record

- "They ask me, 'Are you sure?' They ask me, 'Is it real?'" became actual lines for her parents, with "Again?" first, because she's told them before (#10).
- "The hardest part was knowing I might break somebody's heart" now has Leah upstage, asleep, not knowing.
- The Winter Coat's bridge ("It wasn't some big firework...") moves to after the coat comes off.
- There You Are's bridge ends with Ruth: "There you are."
- "And everybody tells you that you're the lucky one" is unchanged. "Just to play the part, just to avoid the knife!" lost its exclamation point; on stage it's quieter.
- The final rhyme, "ghost / home," is a slant rhyme, but "home" is the word that matters, so it stays.
- Ghost in the Machine's "Error 404: the old man not found" became the button.
