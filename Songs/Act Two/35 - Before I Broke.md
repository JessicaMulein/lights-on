## Notes for the composer

- Slow, intimate piano ballad, minor key, around 68 BPM. A raw, weary vocal that cracks on the high notes. Cello enters at the first chorus; the bridge swells with strings and low toms.
- The next song (#36) answers this one directly. Its bridge starts "Before I broke," so the two have to be in related keys.

## What changed from the record

- It opens on the restructure letter, read by a corporate voice.
- "Then I got sick, and they took my kids / It all came down in the space of a blink" became "Then I got sick, and they took the boys. / It all came down without a noise," a true rhyme.
- "life had hope" now also carries Hope's name, a song after Nora's solo. That's left alone on purpose.
- Nora comes in to take her hand for the last lines, so the audience sees it.
