**Status:** Stage draft 1

**Plan:** A fantasy sequence. She plays the nightmare out loud and takes its power away.

## Notes for the composer

- Strutting outlaw-country brag song: chicken-pickin' Telecaster, walking upright bass, snappy shuffle drums, honky-tonk piano, pedal steel, harmonica stabs. A sly, deadpan vocal with a grin, and comic stop-time breaks before the punchlines.
- The bridge is the thesis of the whole show; give it room.

## What changed from the record

- It's a fantasy now: "Got a letter from the Rock" became "Say a letter comes from the Rock," because the letter never came. The ending says so.
- The Adjuster reads his own line.
- The deadpan spoken outro ("Upload time: five minutes. Claimant is now lying down.") became "It never came. Not yet."
- Everything else is yours.
