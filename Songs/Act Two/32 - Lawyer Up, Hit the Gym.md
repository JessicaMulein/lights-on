## Notes for the composer

- Rowdy pub sea shanty: stomping boots, handclaps, concertina, fiddle, bodhrán, tin whistle, and a big gang-vocal chorus. The comment section is a drunken pub crowd.
- Nora's pinned comment should get the biggest laugh of the night. Give it silence on both sides.

## What changed from the record

- Verse 2's comments are split among individual STRANGERS from the crowd.
- Nora speaks her own pinned comment.
- "and she's the best thing ever happened to me" became "the best thing that ever happened to me," for the singer's breath.
- The record's "rooms / moved" is a slant rhyme. It stays, because it's the sincere moment and nobody will be counting.
