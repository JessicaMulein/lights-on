# Alive in the Breeze

**Act Two · #28** · Sung by JENNIFER  
**Status:** Stage draft 1  
**Source:** "Alive in the Breeze"

**Plan:** Thyroid cancer, there on the old scans all along. The Act One lightbox comes back on.

*(Upstage, the lightbox from Act One flickers on, with the same faint high tone. The same scan. Now a DOCTOR points at it.)*

**JENNIFER**
My biggest fear isn't dying, or facing the dark alone.
It isn't burning in a fire, but the fire inside the bone.
I don't need a crystal ball to see the writing on the wall.
I know where this road is leading. I can hear the final call.

*(A soft, steady military snare. A trumpet plays the theme.)*

I don't want to wallow, but it's more than I can take:
the wailing man down the hall, a ghost I cannot shake,
a tortured cry that only heavy ketamine could break,
a vision that will never clear, a constant ringing in my ear.
I'm forced to live in the shadows of a factory of fear.

I just hope that when it reaches deep inside,
I'll be allowed to step off of the ride,
to claim my death with dignity, to face it standing tall.
Before the fire breaks me, let me answer my own call.
Bury me beneath a tree, right there upon the beach,
where the crashing of the ocean is always in reach.
Let me find my peace. Let me find my release.
Alive in the roots, alive in the breeze.

*(The trumpet theme returns, warmer. JENNIFER looks up at the lightbox.)*

They went back to the old scans, the ones from Leah's year,
and there it was in black and white, as plain as it is here.
I'd been sitting by her bedside, counting every breath she drew,
and the thing that's killing me was on a lightbox in full view.
They checked me for the usual, for every common sign,
but nobody thought to look for this, and it grew there, line by line.

*(French horns, swelling strings.)*

Now I must stare down the fortress alone,
face the heavy cannons mounted in stone.
This is my Fort Wagner, the final wall to climb.
Death with dignity is not a coward's crime!
I take back my surrender! I draw the battle line!

*(The full trumpet theme, soaring and mournful, over strings and snare.)*

Beneath the coastal tree, my resting place.
Salt in the air. Roots in the sand.
I took the ending back into my hand.
Alive in the breeze.

*(A last, distant trumpet note. The sound of waves.)*

## Notes for the composer

- Keep the record's architecture: military snare, a lyrical solo-trumpet theme that develops across the song, French horns and strings in the bridge.
- The lightbox's high tone from #13 should come back exactly as it was.

## What changed from the record

- Verse 3 is rewritten around what really happened. The record's "The scans are still clean today, but tomorrow is unknown / We missed the earliest warnings... too busy tending to another's cancer" became the old scans, Leah's year, the lightbox, and the markers nobody checked. Check the details: it says they checked "for every common sign" but not for this. "I was sitting by her bedside" became "I'd been sitting by her bedside," because the scans they went back to were from after Leah died.
- Verse 2's first line, "I don't wish to wallow, but I'm consumed anyway," became "I don't want to wallow, but it's more than I can take," a true rhyme with "shake" and "break."
- Everything else is yours, word for word.
