## Notes for the composer

- Swamp blues: low, controlled, building to defiant. Slide resonator, droning baritone guitar, foot-stomp kick, upright bass, Hammond swells, around 84 BPM. After Act One's glam, this is the sound telling the audience the second act is different.
- It's sung straight to the audience, house lights at half.

## What changed from the record

- One line: "I didn't write the first commit, / I just refused to let it hide" became "I didn't write the first commit, / but I'm putting my name on mine." That rhymes with "line," and it matches "I own the mistakes" in the next song.
- Everything else is yours as written.
