# Silver Tongues

## Notes for the composer

- This is the center of the show. Jennifer's lines are a raw, slow ballad, while the Lawyers sing smooth, close harmony in another key and tempo, like a polished barbershop quartet. The clash between the two is the point.
- "So ordered" should be the most beautiful chord in the score. That's what makes it so cruel.
- Under "I don't remember all of it," bring back a bar of Keep Clear's cello line (#5). It's the same fog: it started with the accident, and the steroids then and the radiation later made it worse.

## What changed from the record

- On the record this is sung from a dying present ("I sit and watch the clock, I know I'm running out of sand"). Here it's in the moment, 2018.
- Verse 2 is replaced with a new verse of the day the boys were taken, following what you told me: they went on different days, Theo first by car with your cousin, and Owen later by plane. The haze is real too; you don't remember all of it, and the verse says so. Its root is the accident in Keep Clear (#5), so the KEEP CLEAR box glows under this verse and that song's cello line comes back. **The small details are mine:** Theo waving his toy engine at the car window, "old enough to know, and too young to explain," and "I'll leave the light on. Okay?" as what she meant to say. The boys leave one at a time, so their chairs empty one at a time.
- Owen is thirteen: he was gone by late March 2018, when you were in Chicago, and that was before his birthday.
- The Lawyers' chorus is new (legal phrases sung as harmony).
- The outro ("The wolf is just too tired to hunt") is held. It could come back near the end of the show, after Break Me.
- "I own the mistakes" is kept and repeated at the end as the last word, because it's what makes her believable.
