**Status:** Stage draft 1

## Notes for the composer

- Dark outlaw blues stomp built on a chain-gang work song: a hammer on stone on the downbeat, heavy stomps, gritty slide guitar, droning baritone guitar, upright bass, sparse honky-tonk piano. This is the eleven o'clock number, the biggest solo in the show.
- The Good Grief coda changes key and style completely: soft piano, as though the stomp's anger has burned off and left only the decision.

## What changed from the record

- Break Me is unchanged; it was ready.
- "So swing again, I'll scatter: glass and gravel" became "So swing again; I'll scatter / glass and gravel," which is only punctuation.
- The Good Grief walk-off chorus, held back from #25, becomes the coda, with Claire and the football.
