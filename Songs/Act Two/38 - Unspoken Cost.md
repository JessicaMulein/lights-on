# Unspoken Cost

**Act Two · #38** · Sung by JENNIFER, TEEN JEN  
**Status:** Stage draft 1  
**Source:** "Unspoken Cost," with "Road Less Traveled" (#7) in counterpoint

**Plan:** Jennifer and her seventeen-year-old self, in counterpoint.

<!-- lyrics -->

*(Felt piano alone. JENNIFER finds the spiral notebook. She opens it. TEEN JEN is suddenly at a school desk again, writing, three feet away.)*

**JENNIFER**
I found you in a notebook
on the edge of the night,
an English class assignment
with the sources cited right.
They asked for Yeats and Shakespeare,
so you let them hold the door,
and you slipped the truth right past them.
Nobody looked for more.

You said behind you stood a man
with his morals all in place.

**TEEN JEN** *(soft, not hearing her)*
Behind me lies the life of a man...

**JENNIFER**
You never once called him "I," love.
You never gave him your face.
One road was happiness, you wrote,
the other, innocence lost,

**TEEN JEN**
To one side lies happiness at unspoken cost...

**JENNIFER**
and you set the word "unspoken"
right in front of "cost."

You didn't need me to tell you.
You already knew the toll:
the ones who raised you turning cold,
the name, the work, the home,
the stares, the pointing fingers,
the thing you feared you'd be.
You knew exactly what it cost,
and you walked on through to me.

And for five minutes, sweetheart,
nobody looked twice.
I moved through rooms like water.
I was no one, and it was nice.
Then a headache in twenty-eighteen
that nothing else could touch.
They gave me steroids by the handful,
and they gave me far too much.
The face I'd walked through fire for
went somewhere I can't find.
You got your wings for five minutes,
then only in my mind.

Now I hold it through the movie,
and I hold it on the drive.
I count the stick-figure doorways
like it's how I stay alive,
'cause somewhere there's a man who thinks
that room is his to keep,
and you saw him coming too, love,
back when you couldn't sleep.

*(Strings out. Voice and piano. TEEN JEN looks up, as though she almost heard something.)*

You asked the night to hide you,

**TEEN JEN**
Oh, sweet night, hide me in your cloaked arms...

**JENNIFER**
to carry you into the dark.
It did.
You were right about the dark.
You wrote it's nobler to take the arrows

**TEEN JEN**
'Twas nobler to bear the slings and arrows...

**JENNIFER**
than to hide behind a smile.
You were right. I took them,
every single mile.
Three years after the car,
and you wrote better than you knew.

*(Full strings, swelling. The two melodies at once.)*

**JENNIFER**
I won't tell you it was worth it,
and I won't say it was wrong.
You saw every stone along the road,
and you walked it all along.
I'm the one you were afraid you'd be,
and I'm the one you'd choose.
You knew exactly what it cost,
and you walked on through.

**TEEN JEN** *(at the same time)*
Behind me lies the life of a man,
morals intact, skills in demand.
Ahead is a fork in the road,
and I will take the road less traveled.

*(The instruments fall away. For the first time, TEEN JEN looks straight at JENNIFER, and sees her.)*

**TEEN JEN**
Here I stand.

**JENNIFER**
Still here.

## Notes for the composer

- Neoclassical art-pop: felt piano and string quartet with a solo cello countermelody, and a soft electronic pulse in the second half. Intimate, like reading an old letter aloud.
- The final chorus is a real quodlibet: Unspoken Cost and the Road Less Traveled chorus (#7) sung at the same time. Write them so they fit; the bar lengths will need matching.
- Teen Jen's lines through the verses are the exact words of her poem, sung to her tune from #7.

## What changed from the record

- Teen Jen now sings her own poem's lines back whenever Jennifer quotes them.
- The final chorus is in counterpoint with Road Less Traveled.
- The ending is split: "Here I stand" (Teen Jen, the poem's first words) and "Still here" (Jennifer).
- Your lyric is otherwise unchanged.
