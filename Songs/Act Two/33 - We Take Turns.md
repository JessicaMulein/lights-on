## Notes for the composer

- Warm piano ballad: grand piano and Rhodes, upright bass, brushed drums, gentle strings. Unhurried and conversational, with a chorus that lifts.
- This is the full version of the melody first heard in Thirteen (#4) and The Photograph (#19). Let it finally open all the way.

## What changed from the record

- It opens with Nora singing the Thirteen verse from her side ("and you stood against the wall... You still are, after all").
- Verse 4: "and you're scared of where it goes, / so I'll learn to tell you colors. / I'll describe the world I know" became "so I'll learn to tell you colors: / every yellow, every rose," a true rhyme.
- Nora's verse is new: "Don't you make me out a hero." Its second couplet is true: the year you shared a school and barely spoke, she was volunteering on an LGBT center's lifeline, learning how to talk to you. Act One plants it (Scene 6), and this is where the audience finds out. It ends with the lights motif, "you're the one who keeps it on." These are my words for your wife, so check them with her.
- Nora answers the bridge with "I know."
- Verses 1 and 2 aren't repeated, since the audience heard them in Act One.
