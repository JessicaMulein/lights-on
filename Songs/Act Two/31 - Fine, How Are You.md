# Fine, How Are You?

**Act Two · #31** · Sung by JENNIFER, COMPANY (A FRIEND, A PHARMACIST, A NEIGHBOR)  
**Status:** Stage draft 1  
**Source:** "Fine, How Are You?"

**Plan:** Western swing montage, with the Adjuster popping up in the background.

<!-- lyrics -->

*(Bright twin fiddles, a bouncy two-beat swing. A grocery store. JENNIFER with a basket. A FRIEND swoops in for a hug by the frozen peas. Somewhere in the background, THE ADJUSTER pretends to read a cereal box.)*

**JENNIFER**
Ran into an old friend by the frozen peas.
She hugged me hard and said,

**A FRIEND**
Oh, honey, tell me, please,
how've you been? It's been forever! What is new?

**JENNIFER**
So I looked her in the eye, and I told her true:

I'm fine! How are you?
Lost the house, lost the plot,
and the dog moved in with the realtor too.
Oh, I'm fine! Don't you worry,
I'm just peachy through and through.
I'm fine, fine, fine.
How are you?

*(A pharmacy counter. A PHARMACIST counting pills. THE ADJUSTER behind a display of reading glasses.)*

Then the pharmacist was counting out my pills
like a blackjack dealer dealing out a hand of ills.

**A PHARMACIST**
How's your day been, hon?

**JENNIFER**
With a friendly little wink,
and I said, "Oh, gosh, let's see now, I think..."

I'm fine! How are you?
Got a scan lit up like a pinball machine,
and a bill so big it's got a zip code too.
Oh, I'm fine! Don't you worry,
I'm just peachy through and through.
I'm fine, fine, fine.
How are you?

*(Hot fiddle solo, then steel. THE ADJUSTER is caught dancing and stops.)*

*(THE LANDLORD sidles up, tears a page from his ledger, and tucks it into JENNIFER's basket. She reads it: thirty thousand dollars. She folds it very small, puts on her biggest smile, and carries on.)*

*(The sidewalk. A NEIGHBOR flags her down.)*

Then my neighbor flagged me down, said she'd had an awful week:

**A NEIGHBOR**
Broke a nail, the Wi-Fi's slow, and the faucet's got a leak!

**JENNIFER**
So I dropped my bags right there and I took her by the hands,
said, "Oh, sweetie, that's so much. Sit down. I understand."

*(Slows down: voice and steel guitar, sincere. To us.)*

'Cause "fine" is just a door you close real kind,
so nobody has to see the mess inside.
Most folks only want the short reply,
and honey, that's all right.
But if you ask me twice, and you mean it too,
pull up a chair, I'll put the coffee on for you.
It's a long one.
I'll tell you true.

**JENNIFER and COMPANY** *(full swing, extra bright)*
I'm fine! How are you?
Lost the house, lost the plot,
and the dog sends Christmas cards from the realtor's too.
Oh, I'm fine! Don't you worry,
I'm just peachy through and through.
I'm fine, fine, fine...

**JENNIFER** *(stop-time, spoken, gentle, to the audience)*
No, really. How are you?

*(The band kicks back in for a big swing ending.)*

**COMPANY**
Fine, fine, fine!

**JENNIFER** *(spoken, chipper)*
Okay, bye! Love your shoes!

## Notes for the composer

- Bright Western swing: twin fiddles, steel guitar, boogie piano, slapped bass, brushed two-beat drums.
- "Fine" was Louise's word in #3 ("She'd tell you she was fine, then she'd bake you a pie"). Jennifer came by it honestly.

## What changed from the record

- "Lost the house, lost the job" became "lost the house, lost the plot," because at this point in the show the job isn't gone yet (#35).
- The friend, the pharmacist and the neighbor sing their own lines.
- Staged as a montage, with the Adjuster hiding in every location.
- Everything else is as you wrote it.
