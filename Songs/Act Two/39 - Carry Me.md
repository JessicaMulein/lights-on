# Carry Me

**Act Two · #39** · Sung by JENNIFER, NORA, RUTH, CASEY, NORA'S FAMILY, HAL, TESS  
**Status:** Stage draft 1  
**Source:** "Carry Me"

**Plan:** Sung from the edges of the stage. They carry her; they don't fill the chairs.

## Notes for the composer

- Your "They Carry Me" production is the model: cinematic Celtic, with uilleann pipes leading over strings and taiko. On stage, start small, with pipes and voice, and let the people at the edges add one voice at a time.
- Not a big crowd number. Six or seven voices, spread out.

## What changed from the record

- In the bridge, Jennifer names her people, using their stage names.
- Hal raises a hand at "my father," since he's both: blood, and someone who chose her.
- Hal is alive, and so is your sister by blood, who is on stage now as TESS (a stage name). The two siblings who died, Maggie and Sam, are the two lights that go out at "stones that bear their names." The audience met them in Turns Out It's Genetic (#18), so it lands. **The empty light is my idea:** it stands for the brother who was taken by his father and whom you've never found. It's still lit at the end, the same way the lights are left on for the boys. Cut it if it's too much.
- Otherwise your lyric, unchanged.
