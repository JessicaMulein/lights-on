## Notes for the composer

- This is Nora's solo, the one she's been missing. A soaring ballad, but intimate at the start: piano, then strings, building to a full, open final chorus.
- Nora was losing her sight in #33, so let the "lose the light" bridge be hers.

## What changed from the record

- The lyric is yours, nearly word for word. It's now sung by Nora, about her lost daughter. "It's the only name that keeps me on this ground" is now about Hope's name.
- Nora sings the bridge; Jennifer joins her for the last two lines.
- **This is your wife's loss.** Ask her how she wants it told, or whether she wants it told at all.
- The song ends on Nora. An earlier draft added a coda with Jennifer uploading the track and fearing the Adjuster would see it; it's cut, because this is Nora's song, and Scene 35 already carries that fear.
