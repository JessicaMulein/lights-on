# The Crossing

**Act Two · #29** · Sung by JENNIFER, COMPANY (as the souls on the river), LEAH, LOUISE, NORA (offstage); THE FERRYMAN (danced)  
**Status:** Stage draft 1  
**Source:** "The Crossing Dream," "Journeying the Styx" (dance music), and "Adrift on the Styx" (bridge and outro)

**Plan:** Code blue. Dream ballet. Leah sees Jennifer as herself for the first time; what she does is Jennifer's to decide.

<!-- lyrics -->

*(A hospital alarm. A flatline tone that becomes a single harp note. The hospital dissolves into mist and a black river. JENNIFER stands on the shore in a hospital gown.)*

**JENNIFER**
I stand where the living and dead collide,
chaos behind me, peace on the tide.
The weeping of earth on the mortal shore,
but the water is still forevermore.
The mist is heavy, the air is cold,
a dream of a crossing for the brave and the bold.

*(Dark contrabass, sharp harp plucks, a tolling bell. THE FERRYMAN poles out of the fog, a tall figure in gray. He holds out his hand.)*

Through the fog, the hollow prow appears,
carrying the weight of a thousand years.
Ancient Charon holds out his hand
for the silver price to leave this land.
I place the coin in his withered palm,
trading the storm for the endless calm.

*(The boat moves. The COMPANY, as souls at the oars, row in rhythm: sweeping cellos, timpani.)*

**COMPANY**
Row, push through the shadowed deep,
over the river of dreamless sleep.
The dark water ripples, the harp strings cry
beneath the dome of a starless sky.
Row through the sorrow, row through the night,
moving away from the fading light.

*(Dance: "The Dark Oars" from Journeying the Styx. The far shore comes into view. On it, waiting, are LOUISE, in her cardigan, and LEAH, well again, in her flannel.)*

*(The boat touches the far shore. LEAH looks at JENNIFER: at her face, her hair, all of her. For the first time, she sees her.)*

**LEAH** *(spoken, slowly)*
Jen?

**JENNIFER** *(spoken)*
It's me.

*(A long moment. LEAH reaches out, and stops just short of touching her face.)*

**LEAH** *(spoken)*
Go back.

**LOUISE** *(spoken)*
Go on, kiddo. Somebody's calling you.

*(From behind, far off, the way you'd hear someone through a wall: NORA.)*

**NORA** *(offstage, the We Take Turns melody)*
We take turns.
It's my turn.
Come home.

**JENNIFER** *(slowing, rubato; a solo violin)*
Your voice, a phantom through the veil,
a weeping song, a fragile sail.
To cross the river ends the pain,
but leaving you is bitter rain.
I hold the coin. I look across
and weigh the quiet against the loss.

I see the shores of the peaceful gray,
but a tether pulls my soul away.
I dream of the crossing. I long for the rest,
but the mortal breath beats in my chest.
It is not my time to step on the sand.
I must let go of the ferryman's hand.

*(She lets go. THE FERRYMAN withdraws. LEAH and LOUISE fade into the mist.)*

I turn my back on the quiet deep.
I choose the waking. I leave the sleep.
I pull the thread through the heavy night,
away from the ferryman's fading light.
I take the breath. I take the pain.
I come back to your arms again.

*(A gasp: the first breath. The hospital alarm resolves into a steady beep.)*

## Notes for the composer

- The one place the show leaves realism: a dream ballet. Cinematic orchestra, heavy concert harp, sweeping dark strings and timpani, and wordless choir. Draw the dance score from Journeying the Styx, but keep the dance to one movement (about a minute and a half) rather than all six, and set the Crossing Dream lyrics over it. The scene needs the river, not the whole voyage.
- Nora's three lines use the We Take Turns melody, coming from behind the audience if the theater allows it. The full song doesn't come until #33, but the phrase is planted in Scene 23, when Nora sits with Jennifer at the headache clinic ("We take turns."), so the audience already knows it's something they say. The song pays it off later.

## What changed from the record

- Stitched together from three pieces: the Crossing Dream lyrics (movements 1 to 4 and the oars chorus), Journeying the Styx as dance music, and Adrift on the Styx's bridge and outro.
- **The far-shore moment is new, and the choice is yours.** In this draft Leah sees her and says "Jen?" She never calls her by any name of her own in Act One, because she's in denial, so this is the first time since the party. It's the in-between name, so it can be read either way. Then she tells her to go back. Another option: Leah says "Jennifer," and "Say it again" from The Party (#9) returns. A third: Leah says nothing and turns away. Each one tells a different story about her.
- Louise's "Go on, kiddo" echoes her "Come on, kiddo" from #3.
- Nora's "We take turns. It's my turn. Come home." is new.
- It happened as you told it: in the psych unit, before the surgery, after the cancer got into your head and you'd stopped eating and drinking. You were alone, so Nora isn't in the room. The nurse finds you and calls the code, and Nora's voice reaches you only in the dream, "through the veil." She arrives in Six Month Bites.
