## Notes for the composer

- Mid-tempo and warm, with a little swing in it: the first time Act Two breathes. Acoustic guitar, piano, light drums.
- "Still here" at the end is spoken, and it answers Curtains Up's "I'm still here!" from the end of Act One.

## What changed from the record

- The Nurse speaks now: "There you are" (Ruth's words from #15, coming back) and "Come on, kiddo" (Louise's, since the same actor plays both).
- Chorus: "Every single day with my wife by my side / Another page written in the book of life" became "another chapter written, and the book's still open wide," a true rhyme with "side."
- Verse 2: "Bob Barker smile, trying to make it real" became "a game-show smile while they wheel me in." That keeps the Price Is Right joke without naming a real person, and "spin / in" rhymes.
- Small grammar fixes: "the gent" became "the man," and "taking its toll" became "taking their toll."
