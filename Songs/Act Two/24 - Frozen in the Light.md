# Frozen in the Light

**Act Two · #24** · Sung by JENNIFER  
**Status:** Stage draft 1  
**Source:** "Frozen in the Light"

**Plan:** Birthdays. The Act One lullaby returns, broken.

*(A cake with one candle. JENNIFER alone at the table. Upstage, OWEN and THEO appear, frozen at the ages they were when they left: thirteen and three. They don't move.)*

**JENNIFER**
Every year your birthday comes,
and I light a candle all the same.
The calendar says you're grown now,
but you're still small inside the frame.
Somewhere someone with your name
is blowing candles out tonight,
and I'm grieving my baby and his brother,
frozen in the light.

You can't bury someone breathing,
so the funeral never ends.
There's no stone to lay my flowers on,
no casseroles from friends.
Every time I think it's scarred,
the date comes round again,
and I lose you like the first time.
The funeral never ends.

*(Under the next verse, the cello plays the lullaby from #11, slow and out of shape.)*

I try to picture you taller,
with a deeper voice, a stranger's face,
but my mind just hands me back
the two of you in the same place:
my baby in his little train bed,
who never left my side,
and his brother growing up too fast
with a grief too big to hide.

You can't bury someone breathing,
so the funeral never ends.
There's no stone to lay my flowers on,
no casseroles from friends.
Every time I think it's scarred,
the date comes round again,
and I lose you like the first time.
The funeral never ends.

And people say, "At least they're living,"
and I know that that is true,
but in my head you haven't aged a day,
the way only the dead do.
Hope keeps the wound from closing.
It pulls the stitches every spring.
And I'd rather bleed forever
than stop hoping anything.

*(Strings out: voice and piano only. She speaks to the frozen boys.)*

I'm not telling you to hurt you.
None of this is yours to hold.
I just need you to know you were missed
every year that you got old.

You can't bury someone breathing,
so the funeral never ends.
There's no stone to lay my flowers on,
no casseroles from friends.
Every time I think it's scarred,
the date comes round again,
and I lose you like the first time.
The funeral never ends.

*(Cello alone.)*

Keep growing,
even where I can't see.
I'll keep grieving.
That's a trade I'd make again.

*(She hums the lullaby. It breaks off before the last line.)*

Little train, little train...
I'll leave a light on at the station...

*(She can't finish it. She blows out the candle.)*

## Notes for the composer

- Stark neoclassical ballad: solo cello lead, felt piano, chamber strings. Restrained.
- The lullaby (#11) lives inside verse 2 on the cello, bent out of shape, and comes back at the end in her voice, unfinished. "Follow the line" stays unsung until the finale.

## What changed from the record

- The lyric is unchanged; it was already a scene.
- Added: the boys frozen upstage at thirteen and three, the cello lullaby under verse 2, and the broken lullaby at the end.
- "Hope keeps the wound from closing" now also means Hope, who comes in #34. Leave it; the double meaning lands on a second viewing.
