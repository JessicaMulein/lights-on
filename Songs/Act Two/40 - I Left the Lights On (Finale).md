# I Left the Lights On (Finale)

**Act Two · #40** · Sung by JENNIFER, NORA  
**Status:** Stage draft 1  
**Source:** "I Left the Lights On," with Louise's chorus phrase (#3) and the lullaby (#11)

**Plan:** Two women at a table of empty chairs. Louise's "thirty-two years" returns. House lights up.

<!-- lyrics -->

*(JENNIFER and NORA sit side by side. Every other chair is empty: Louise's, Leah's, the small one for Hope, the Whitcombs', Claire's, and the two by the door, set with plates: Owen's and Theo's. The hook by the door is empty. A few soft celesta notes, like the music box.)*

**JENNIFER** *(to the two chairs by the door)*
It was never your fault, not any of it.
You were never too much to keep.
And I never stopped loving you, not for a day,
not awake and not asleep.
They took the chance to tell you,
so I said it the only way:
one song at a time, and I left them here
for whenever you find your way.

And you won't have to knock.
There'll be nobody home.
But I left the lights on
in every song I wrote.
Whatever you were told,
whatever you decide,
it's all still here, love.
Come inside.

*(Louise's melody, from #3. NORA hums it under her.)*

She'd say Walt was waiting, just like he said he'd be,
and thirty-two years was nothing to eternity.
Well, I'm waiting too, as long as I'm allowed,
and when I'm not, the songs will wait.
They're waiting now.

*(Strings out. Voice and piano.)*

I know what it is to knock on a door
and hear nobody come,
so I built you a house out of all that I was,
and I left every light on.

*(Strings return, warm, not loud. One by one, the windows of the house light up. YOUNG JEN and TEEN JEN appear in two of them.)*

**JENNIFER and NORA**
So you won't have to knock.
There'll be nobody home.
But I left the lights on
in every song I wrote.
If you came late, you're not too late.
Whatever you decide,
it's all still here, love.
Come inside.

*(The music box plays the lullaby. JENNIFER sings it, very simply, to the two empty chairs. NORA takes the harmony.)*

**JENNIFER**
Little train, little train,
running through the night,
past the hill and past the river,
past the last streetlight.

**JENNIFER and NORA**
When you wake up, I'll be here.
When you're grown, you'll still be mine.
I'll leave a light on at the station.
Follow the line.

*(Piano alone.)*

**JENNIFER**
The lights are on.
Come in.

*(Instead of a blackout, the house lights come up, slowly, all the way, on the audience. The stage stays lit too. Nobody turns anything off. The door stays open.)*

## Notes for the composer

- Mature cinematic piano ballad: grand piano and a warm string quartet with solo cello. Honest and steady, no melodrama. The final chorus is warm and full, but never loud.
- Three melodies come home here: I Left the Lights On, Louise's "thirty-two years" phrase, and the lullaby. "Follow the line" is sung for the first time since #11. It broke off in #24, and now it finishes.
- No button and no bow chord. The music ends on the piano, and the house lights do the rest.

## What changed from the record

- Verses 1 and 2 were the Prologue, so the finale starts at verse 3.
- New: the Louise verse ("Well, I'm waiting too, as long as I'm allowed, / and when I'm not, the songs will wait. / They're waiting now."). Those are my words, set to her melody.
- New: the lullaby reprise and the house-lights ending.
- "There'll be nobody home" stays, though it's hard to hear. It's the truest line in the song.
