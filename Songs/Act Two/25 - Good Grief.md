# Good Grief

**Act Two · #25** · Sung by JENNIFER, CLAIRE (NORA)  
**Status:** Stage draft 1  
**Source:** "Good Grief"

**Plan:** Claire's promises; the Iowa house sold; Seattle. End on "and nothing was there"; save "Keep the football" for #36.

## Notes for the composer

- Dramatic piano-driven rock ballad: pounding grand piano, cello and strings, drums from sparse to heavy. Simmering verses, a tense pre-chorus, and an explosive chorus.
- The football is a real prop, and each "pull" is staged. Keep it funny the first time and devastating by the third.
- Claire's lines are sweet and sincere. She isn't a villain in her own head.

## What changed from the record

- Claire sings her own promises ("A couple months...") and adds "Next month. I swear."
- Verse 4, the roughest one ("Now you sleep sound in your perfect life, / 'cause I've run myself empty, / blowin' out his candles with the breath from my fall, / knocked on my back by the know-it-all"), became "Now you sleep fine in a house full of noise, / and I sleep in a house with none. / I blow out the candles on their birthdays / for the both of them, one by one."
- It ends on "and nothing was there." The walk-off chorus ("Keep the football, Lucy...") moves to the end of Break Me (#36).
